Why we exist

The biggest threats now sit in the hardware.

For a decade the fight over control was a software fight. It has moved down the stack. A camera on a truck, a band on a wrist, a relay on a pole: each carries chips, radios and firmware that someone else chose, from a supply chain nobody in the room can see.

The phone in your pocket shows where that ends: a sealed stack you rent and never own. We think any organisation that runs critical work should own its hardware the way it already owns its data.

Arab American Instruments is a hardware design company. We own our designs, publish what we learn, and build purpose built devices for each customer, so the customer leaves with the design files, the parts list and the keys.
