Solar Relay

Bolts onto a pole, runs on the sun and passes data pole to pole until it reaches one with a real uplink.

Reference conceptRequest this build

Renders for Solar Relay are in progress. This page describes the reference concept as designed today; parts are shortlisted, not final.

What it does

Inside

  • LoRa gateway for nearby bands and sensors
  • Long range LoRa link to the next pole on a directional antenna
  • Health reports: battery, solar and temperature
  • White, shaded, sealed box with passive vents
  • Uplink build adds cellular on a high gain antenna, or satellite, or microwave

Reference specification

PowerAbout 80 to 100 W solar and about 300 Wh of heat tolerant LiFePO4
Bandwidth300 wearers need under 1 kbit/s; a thin satellite link carries it easily

Figures marked estimate are design targets, set conservatively, and will be confirmed in field trials. Every unit is adapted to the customer, so the delivered specification may differ.
