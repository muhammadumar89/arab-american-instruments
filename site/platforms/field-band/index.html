Sensing · LoRa wrist band for people in the field

Field Band

A slim, sealed band that reports position, heart rate and skin temperature over LoRa, calls for help with one button and lasts days on a charge.

In designRequest this build

From the Design Lab

What it does

What it reports

  • Position from a low power GPS with a thin patch antenna
  • Heart rate and skin temperature, chosen for accuracy on a moving wrist
  • Falls and stillness from a motion sensor
  • Battery level and an off wrist alert

When something goes wrong

  • A recessed SOS button that goes out on every path at once
  • Vibration only once help has confirmed the message
  • A separation alarm when the wearer loses their group
  • A hidden light ring that glows only in an emergency, so rescuers can find the wearer at night

Two builds

  • LoRa only: the slimmest band, about 5 to 7 days per charge (estimate)
  • LoRa plus soldered eSIM: calls for help alone wherever there is phone signal, about 2 days per charge (estimate)

Reference specification

RadioProcessor with built in LoRa; optional cellular with eSIM
Message24 bytes every 5 minutes, instantly for SOS and falls, encrypted per band
BoardsMain board about 4 by 2.7 cm, sensor board about 2 by 2 cm (current estimates)
Battery150 to 250 mAh cell with protection, temperature limited charging and a fuse
SealingIP67, button included; charging contacts are dead off the dock

Figures marked estimate are design targets, set conservatively, and will be confirmed in field trials. Every unit is adapted to the customer, so the delivered specification may differ.
