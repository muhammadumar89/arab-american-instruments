Small decisions on a small board

Why the top of the band's board is nearly empty, and what a board colour costs.

Look at the main board of the Field Band and the top is almost bare. That is on purpose. The GPS antenna is a thin patch, and anything switching nearby makes it deaf. So the electronics live underneath and the top is kept clear for the sky.

The sensor board sits at the very bottom, against the skin, where the heart rate sensor and the charging contacts need to be. Today it is about 2 by 2 cm and the main board about 4 by 2.7 cm. Those numbers will move as the requirements settle.

Colour is a choice too. A black solder mask costs about $3.30 more per board than the standard green. It photographs beautifully. Inside a sealed shell nobody sees it, so for a production run green is usually the right answer, and the customer decides.

We write these down because the customer owns the design. Every one of these choices is in the files they keep.
