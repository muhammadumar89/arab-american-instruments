About

A hardware design company for sovereign systems.

Low-power edge devices for sensing, Satcoms and RT controls in Air Gapped Environments.

The idea

Counterbalance the sealed stack.

Technology keeps concentrating. More and more of what runs a country, a fleet or a field sits on hardware designed by a few companies, sealed so that the people who rely on it cannot look inside or change it.

We think that is a risk every organisation should be able to remove. Arab American Instruments designs edge devices for sensing, satellite and mesh links, and real time control, and builds them so that the customer owns the design and the supply chain, not us and not anyone else.

We do not chase a catalogue. We keep reference designs, publish what we learn, and build purpose built hardware for each customer, from a single pilot to a national fleet.

The bench

Engineers who own the whole board.

Schematic, layout, firmware and shell under one roof, so every decision on the device is one we can explain and hand over.
