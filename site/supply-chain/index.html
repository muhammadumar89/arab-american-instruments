Supply chain

Own the stack, down to the part.

Most edge devices arrive as sealed boxes from a supply chain you cannot see. Ours arrive with their design, their parts list and their keys.

How we work

We own the design. You own the supply chain.

We work the way a design house does. We keep and improve our reference designs, publish what we learn in the Design Lab, and for each customer we build a purpose built device on top of them. When the work is done, the customer holds everything needed to build it again without us.

  1. 01

    Requirement

    What must be sensed, sent or controlled, where, for how long, and what may never leave the site.

  2. 02

    Reference design

    We start from the closest platform and show you the tradeoffs: battery, range, size, cost.

  3. 03

    Your board

    We adapt the schematic, layout and shell to your vehicles, your heat and your rules.

  4. 04

    Pilot run

    A small batch built and tested with you, with every part traced to its maker.

  5. 05

    Field trial

    Units in the field, measured against the targets we set together.

  6. 06

    Handover

    Design files, bill of materials, firmware source and keys. Build the rest wherever you trust.
